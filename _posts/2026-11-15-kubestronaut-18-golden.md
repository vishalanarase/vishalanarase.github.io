This is the last post on [The Road to Golden Kubestronaut](/posts/kubestronaut-00-introduction). [LFCS](/posts/kubestronaut-17-lfcs) was the last exam. If every current CNCF certification is active, and LFCS is active with them, you are a Golden Kubestronaut.

CNCF emails you within about a business week. The rules live on the [program page](https://www.cncf.io/training/kubestronaut/) and the [FAQ](https://www.cncf.io/training/kubestronaut/kubestronaut-faq/). [My profile](https://www.cncf.io/training/kubestronaut/?_sft_lf-country=in&_sfm_lf_person_golden=1&p=vishal-anarase&_sf_s=vishal+anarase) is the listing those emails lead to. Yours will be too.

## What the title includes

From the program page, a Golden Kubestronaut receives:

- An exclusive backpack with the Golden Kubestronaut logo
- A Golden Kubestronaut beanie
- A ThriveOne subscription, for as long as the Kubestronaut title is maintained
- One free ticket per year to a Kubernetes Community Days event
- One 50% off KubeCon registration per year
- A 60% discount voucher for each new CNCF certification
- A Golden listing on the Kubestronaut website

The backpack is the souvenir. The voucher matters more. When CNCF adds an exam, that voucher is how you sit it.

## How the title stays yours

Every certification has to be unexpired. One lapsed exam is enough to lose the title until you renew it.

The associate and professional exams on this road are valid for two years. Renewal is a retake, finished before the expiration date. A few CARE rules shorten that work:

- Passing CKA or CKAD renews KCNA.
- Passing CKS renews KCSA.
- Earning or recertifying CKS on or after 18 June 2026 extends CKA to the same expiration date.

Put the expiration dates in one list the week you finish. Renew the one that expires first. Do not wait for a reminder from the jacket.

## When CNCF adds an exam

The Golden list grows. A new CNCF certification joins the requirements after it reaches general availability, and not earlier than 90 days after that. CNPA joined on 15 October 2025. CNPE joined on 1 March 2026. The next one will be announced on the program page. The 60% voucher is for that exam. Book it inside the window, and keep the rest of the list current while you study.

## What you can hand to the next person

Sixteen exams is still a wall, until it is a path. You walked it one exam at a time. The posts on this road are the map: the syllabus, the docs, and the next exam.

Send the next person to the [introduction](/posts/kubestronaut-00-introduction). Tell them to start with KCNA. Tell them Kubestronaut is a real finish line, and Golden is the decision to keep going.

Take the evening off. Then put the expiration dates on a calendar.
