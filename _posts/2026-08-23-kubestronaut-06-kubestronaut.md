This is the pause on [The Road to Golden Kubestronaut](/posts/kubestronaut-00-introduction). [CKS](/posts/kubestronaut-05-cks) was the fifth Kubernetes exam. If KCNA, CKA, CKAD, KCSA, and CKS are all current, you are a Kubestronaut.

Stop here if you want to. Five exams is a finish line. The Golden half of the road is a choice you make after you have felt what finishing feels like.

## What you just did

You learned the words ([KCNA](/posts/kubestronaut-01-kcna)). You ran a cluster ([CKA](/posts/kubestronaut-02-cka)). You built what runs on it ([CKAD](/posts/kubestronaut-03-ckad)). You learned the security ideas in calm conditions ([KCSA](/posts/kubestronaut-04-kcsa)). Then you hardened the cluster with your hands ([CKS](/posts/kubestronaut-05-cks)).

That is the whole Kubernetes certification catalog. CNCF emails you within about a business week after the last required certification is active. The live rules are on the [program page](https://www.cncf.io/training/kubestronaut/) and the [FAQ](https://www.cncf.io/training/kubestronaut/kubestronaut-faq/).

Every one of the five has to be unexpired. A lapsed CKA is enough to lose the title until you renew it. Passing or recertifying CKS on or after 18 June 2026 extends CKA to the same date, under the CARE program. Passing CKA or CKAD renews KCNA. Passing CKS renews KCSA. Use those links. They are how the title stays yours while you decide what comes next.

## What the title includes

From the program page, a Kubestronaut receives:

- An exclusive jacket
- A Credly badge
- Access to the private Kubestronaut Slack channel and mailing list
- Five coupons for 50% off certifications each year, for yourself or to share
- 20% off three CNCF events a year, KubeCon or KubeDays

The jacket is the visible part. The coupons are the practical part. If you continue toward Golden, those discounts are how the next exams get cheaper.

## If you keep going

Golden Kubestronaut is every current CNCF certification, plus LFCS. The next exams are one subject at a time: Prometheus, OpenTelemetry, Cilium, Istio, GitOps, Argo, Backstage, Kyverno, the platform exams, and Linux last.

Take the evening off before you open the next page. Then start with metrics. You cannot operate what you cannot see.
